Not picked by hand.

Chroma Lab uses K-means clustering to discover groups of similar colors in an image. No labels or pretrained model are needed.

  1. Sample. Read up to 8,000 pixels from your image.
  2. Group. Convert colors to OKLab, then repeatedly assign pixels to their nearest color center and move each center to its group’s average.
  3. Repaint. Replace every pixel with its nearest discovered color.

OKLab helps color distances better reflect how we see. Percentages show each color’s share of the processed image. Clustering stops after convergence or 30 rounds.

Images are processed locally, resized to a maximum 1,200-pixel edge, and flattened on white if transparent. Exports use this processed size. Results are deterministic for the same image and color count. The sample is AI-generated.
